How booking works

  1. Pick a campaign

    Compare Gold, Silver and Economy and choose the one that fits.

  2. Fill in your details

    Enter your information, trip type and guide language.

  3. Get your confirmation

    Your booking is saved and shown in the bookings list.

Our vision

Pilgrims need a single platform to book Hajj and Umrah campaigns, with clear details (features, services, price, start and end dates). They should be able to upload required documents linked with Tawakkalna and communicate directly with campaign organizers through the app.
